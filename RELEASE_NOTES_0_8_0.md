# Daniel's Frontier 0.8.0 — combat update

Combat, weapon effects and biological barrage update.

- Enemies use attack runs, strafing and artillery repositioning instead of constant circling. Damage, durability, XP, credits and equipment drops have been retuned to slow progression while retaining first-drop and boss guarantees.
- Shield and hull damage now share one conserved hit budget. Covered player hulls receive less leakage; depleted shields pass the uncovered remainder to hull.
- Red outgoing and incoming damage numbers, critical emphasis and clear MISS feedback show actual damage dealt.
- Lasers and rockets fire through the weapons present on each model. Grouped equipment slots retain their individual combat results. Ordinary lasers use the selected wispy ribbon, with fitted-DPS colors and intensity; enemies use a narrower palette.
- Biological enemies use six weaker organic projectiles per barrage, emerging from their model's mouth with separated trajectories, varied surface impacts, droplets and mist. Their total rolled volley budget is preserved; biological enemies carry no rockets.
- Player ships turn and bank smoothly, with the selected cyan-white engine plumes aligned to their authored nozzles.
- The in-game encyclopedia reflects the revised combat and drop rules.

The accepted 0.7.9 menu, artwork, equipment UI and dependency notices remain the baseline. Steam integration, new art studies and unfinished legal activation are outside this update.
