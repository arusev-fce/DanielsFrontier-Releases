# Daniel's Frontier — Game and Community Rules

Version 0.8.2 - 6 November 2026

## Accounts and fair play

Online accounts are for players aged 16 or over. Choose a public pilot name
that does not impersonate someone else. Protect your account and do not sell
or share it. Offline pilots and online progression are separate.

Do not steal accounts, attack infrastructure, tamper with authoritative state,
duplicate rewards, exploit errors or use unauthorized online automation.
Report bugs and security concerns to support@contact.bratochki.com. A report is not
permission to disrupt the service or test against another player's account.

## Resources and random equipment

Credits, Aurum, Keys, Daniel Orbs, scrap and equipment are earned game resources.
The game has no advertisements, real-money purchases, official player item
marketplace or cash redemption. NPC sales award game Credits, not money.
Account sales, real-money item trades and outside betting through the service
are prohibited.

The Exquisite exchange costs **1 earned Daniel Orb** for one crate. Its rarity
threshold is **80% Epic / 20% Legendary**. The outcome is determined from the
crate's identity; reconnecting or repeating a request does not produce a new
entitlement. Equipment type and attributes follow the rarity's equipment-roll
rules. A particular named item is not guaranteed, and a small set of openings
need not match the long-run rarity proportions.

Boss Mythic reward protection applies to qualifying committed boss-equipment
rewards. It does not guarantee a Mythic Exquisite Crate reward or a Mythic
after a number of crate openings. Normal-drop protection is also separate.

Administrators may correct errors in progression, inventory or balances with
a recorded reason and audit trail. These corrections are not paid services.

## Chat and conduct

Sector chat is visible to other players receiving it. Do not post passwords,
recovery codes, private contact details or someone else's personal information.

The following are prohibited:

- harassment, targeted abuse, discriminatory slurs, threats or incitement;
- sexual messages or images, solicitation or exploitation;
- doxxing, impersonation, scams, malware or account-selling advertisements;
- spam, deliberate disruption and evasion of restrictions.

Keep language respectful and avoid profanity and graphic descriptions. Report
abuse to support@contact.bratochki.com, giving the pilot name, approximate time/sector and
a brief description. Send only necessary evidence and remove unrelated private
information. Do not forward unlawful material; describe the issue first.

Chat is not continuously supervised. The operator can warn, disconnect or ban
players, taking account of severity, repetition and context. Serious risks
can require immediate action. You may request human review at the support
address. Do not create another account to evade a restriction.

## Content submissions and changes

The current game has no public player-decal upload service. Do not assume
that sending a file grants permission to publish it or obliges us to use it.
Any future submission feature will state its content, rights and privacy rules
before accepting submissions.

Material rule and mechanic changes are explained in dated updates. These rules
operate with the [EULA](EULA.md) and [Privacy Notice](PRIVACY_NOTICE.md).
