# Daniel's Frontier 0.8.9.5 - Fleet, rewards and interface improvements

- Engine fitting limits: Wayfarer 2, Vanguard 3, Peregrine 4, Bastion 5, later ships 6. Equip Best and server validation enforce them; excess equipment is retained safely.
- Final movement cap 50 ls/s, including boosts. Diminishing returns start above 25; acceleration is more responsive, with a 0.12-0.20-second base ramp and brief momentum retention between rapid steering updates. Explicit Stop remains immediate.
- Stronger Map 1 enemies and Map 2 ordinary enemies, improved firing ranges and early melee damage. Main-map pursuit ends after eight seconds without a damaging hit. Portal combat remains unchanged.
- Revised elite equipment chances and map-specific rarities; remove the additional boss promotion roll. Map bosses add 1/2/3 Mythic-pity points toward 100; existing progress is retained. One equipment roll per group kill.
- Block duplicate equipment claims across pending rewards, inbox recovery and world crates.
- Revised shop prices, reduced 5% from the initial reviewed candidate. Shop descriptions match actual resolved equipment stats; Buy is bound to the displayed item. Common starter gear remains available.
- Revised resale values, up to 50% level/star premium on dropped gear, 25% recorded-spending shop refunds and capped crafting recovery. No Engine/Utility resale penalties.
- Faster shop/inventory refresh paths, auxiliary rack priority in Equip Best, step-only quantity fields and corrected Buy 1 Daniel Chest wording.
- Main-map Aurum rewards increased toward modeled ordinary-hunting incomes of approximately 4k/9k/15k per hour. Actual income depends on encounters, equipment, travel and competition.
- Map 2/3 mining output +25%/+50%; regional hire and upgrade cost factors 1.75x/2.75x. Existing miners retained; eight-hour offline limit unchanged. Ore tooltips avoid repeated off-target work.
- Brightness slider 0.5-2.0; movable/reorderable HEX dock with hide/restore. Hotkeys: I Inventory, M Map, B Shop, K Skills, C Workshop, X Expeditions, F Friends, H Hangar, E Collect.
- Encyclopedia drop odds and pity descriptions updated. Matching private admin console/catalog included.

Includes matching Windows client, server and private owner console.
