# Daniel's Frontier 0.8.9.6 - Expedition rewards and smoother interfaces

- Expeditions pay substantially larger Credit, Aurum and upgrade-material rewards. Normal, Hard and Elite Credit entry fees are lower. Every builder completion awards Epic-or-better equipment, with stronger Legendary/Mythic chances on higher difficulties. Helpers receive separate completion bonuses.
- Portal costs use the same compact resource tiles as crafting, with currencies first and exact owned/required amounts on hover.
- Faster shop browsing and ship previews: background texture loading, cached models/materials and fewer repeated layout calculations.
- Skill hints reuse one panel while zooming. Hotbar, item, pin and window hints avoid repeated updates; redundant Event Log and resize hints are removed.
- Smoother drone turning, catch-up and formation settling. Original ship engine trails are preserved.
- Untouched enemies stop automatically following into protected gates; enemies you damage can retaliate. Provocation clears when they disengage or respawn.
- Ember Raider, Lancer and Bomber track moving targets more consistently, with smoother strafing and adjusted firing reach. Portal enemies and boss/miniboss combat stats are unchanged.
- Revised regional-material requirements for crafted equipment, cloak, shield burst, jump drive and core synthesis. Mining production, ore costs and stabilizer recipes are unchanged.
- Matching Windows client, server and private admin console. Existing pilots and equipment are preserved.
