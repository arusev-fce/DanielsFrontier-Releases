# Daniel's Frontier 0.8.2 - Fleet and interface update

Release 6 November 2026. Client and server version0.8.2.

## Less time managing equipment

Drag modules into matching sockets, or double-click/Ctrl-click to install and uninstall. Compact Best, Install/Uninstall, Remove all, Sell and Sort actions keep the cargo view clear. Item cards separate totals from bonuses, with distinct names and clearer explanations.

## Your rewards, kept together

Overflow equipment waits in the reward inbox. Standard rewards expire after30days; administrator deliveries never expire. Claims need cargo room and cannot duplicate items. Collector, radar, auxiliary-rack and repair systems now have one-per-ship limits; displaced gear is preserved.

## Faster entry and clearer alerts

Optionally remember credentials securely on your Windows account. Sign in and enter directly. Styled orange notices highlight group invitations, rewards, skill points and new cargo. Connection status and a clearer server-loss message make interruptions easier to understand.

## Build your fleet

Four icon-inspired drone models include the new Wasp and Bulwark tiers. Wing improves damage/accuracy; Wheel exchanges20%shield capacity for20%speed, subject to the final speed cap. Thirty permanent mob-kill ranks have distinct insignia and a progression guide.

## Combat you can read

Engines stay brighter through smooth turns and recover faster. Lasers and toxic shots are wider. BIO volleys can miss individual projectiles, and spores launch from their green glands. Close-range salvage enemies gain an articulated grip strike; the circular Siege Core uses its authored weapon banks without multiplying damage.

## A clearer journey

Home encounters pay more toward early ships, Rift keeps a similar combined attack budget and Ember is more dangerous. Existing map artwork has stronger parallax with unchanged world bounds. Internal panels and ESC use the game's main-menu styling.

Optional support reports include more timing and effect counters to help distinguish connection delays from client hitches. Updated privacy/EULA notices describe this behavior and saved credentials. No claim that all hardware/network lag is eliminated.
